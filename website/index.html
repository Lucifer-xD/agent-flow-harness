Features

Built for the long sessions.

The parts that make an agent dependable when the job takes hours, not minutes.

◎

Goals

The agent sets its own goal from your request and keeps going until it's met — or tells you what's blocking it. Achieved goals are kept under the Goal button.

☰

A to-do list you can touch

Every step shows live. Add, reorder or tick items yourself and the agent sees your edits on its next turn.

⇶

Subagents in parallel

A built-in Helper plus your own agents. Watch any of them work from the top bar, see what the lead asked and what came back.

◉

Live file pane

Tool arguments stream as they're generated, so you see code appear character by character — not after the fact.

↺

Rewind anything

Undo to any message: the thread and the files on disk go back together, and your message returns to the box to edit and resend.

⑂

Git worktrees

Give a thread or a helper its own worktree, then merge it back when you're happy. Your main branch stays clean.

∞

Endless threads

Huge histories are summarised in parts, in place — old messages stay readable, the model reads the summary. Runs cut off by a restart resume by themselves.

⚑

Hooks, commands, jobs

Run checks before or after any tool, add your own slash commands, and let long processes like dev servers run in the background.

$

Cost you can see

Tokens and cost per thread, budgets that stop a run, prompt caching on by default for Anthropic models.

ع

English & Arabic

A full Arabic interface with right-to-left layout, switchable live — plus 25 themes and a one-click light/dark toggle.

⌘

MCP & skills

Plug in MCP servers and skills, and bring your threads over from OpenCode or Zadloop in one click.

⌂

Yours, locally

Everything runs and is stored on your PC. Your keys, your providers, your files — no account needed.

Models

Bring any model.
Mix them in one thread.

19 providers built in, plus any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Use a strong model to lead and a fast one to help — context sizes, prices and reasoning levels come straight from models.dev.
