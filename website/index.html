Features

Built for the long sessions.

The parts that make an agent dependable when the job takes hours, not minutes.

◎

Goals

The agent sets its own goal from your request and keeps going until it's met — or tells you what's blocking it. Achieved goals are kept under the Goal button.

❖

It remembers your project

What the agent learns — how to run and test it, your conventions, your preferences — goes into the project's memory, and every new thread starts knowing it. See and edit it from the Memory button.

☰

A to-do list you can touch

Every step shows live. Add, reorder or tick items yourself and the agent sees your edits on its next turn.

⇶

Subagents in parallel

A built-in Helper plus your own agents. Watch any of them work from the top bar, see what the lead asked and what came back.

↺

Watch it, rewind it

Code streams into the live file pane as it's written. Undo to any message and the thread and the files on disk go back together.

▣

From your phone

The AFH app for Android and iPhone: chat with any model using your own keys, and take over AFH on your computer from anywhere — live threads, one-tap approvals, new tasks.

⑂

Git worktrees

Give a thread or a helper its own worktree, then merge it back when you're happy. Your main branch stays clean.

∞

Endless threads

Huge histories are summarised in parts, in place — old messages stay readable, the model reads the summary. Runs cut off by a restart resume by themselves.

⚑

Hooks, commands, MCP

Run checks before or after any tool, add your own slash commands and skills, plug in MCP servers, and keep dev servers running in the background.

$

Cost you can see

Tokens and cost per thread, budgets that stop a run, prompt caching on by default for Anthropic models.

ع

English & Arabic

A full Arabic interface with right-to-left layout, switchable live — plus 46 themes and a one-click light/dark toggle.

⌂

Yours, locally

Everything runs and is stored on your PC. Your keys, your providers, your files — no account needed (one is optional, for the phone app).
