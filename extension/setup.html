Add AFH to your browser

You do this once. After that AFH works in this browser by itself, in a tab group of its own, with nothing to allow each time.

  1. Open the extensions page

    Browsers only open this page when you go there yourself. Click the address bar (Ctrl L), paste, and press Enter.

    chrome://extensions
  2. Turn on Developer mode

    The switch is at the top right of that page (at the left in Edge).

    Developer mode
  3. Drag the AFH folder onto that page

    AFH opened a window with the folder browser-extension selected: drag it onto the extensions page. Or click Load unpacked and choose this folder:

That's all. Settings → Agent → Your Chrome turns green in AFH, and you can close this tab. The extension updates together with AFH.
