# Code of Conduct

Agent Flow Harness is for everyone. We want taking part in this project to be a friendly, respectful experience, whatever your background or level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Welcome newcomers and be patient with questions.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## What's not okay

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning language of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and other project spaces, and whenever you represent the project in public.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, use GitHub's [Report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) option on the comment or profile. Maintainers will review reports and treat them with care.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or block anyone who breaks these rules.

This code is based on the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
